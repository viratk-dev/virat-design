# More Formats

## Posters & Flyers

- Sizes: A-series for print (A3 3508×4961, A4 2480×3508 at 300dpi) or 1080×1350 for digital.
- Hero-led: one giant headline or visual, minimal supporting copy.
- Information hierarchy: what → when/where → action. In that order, decreasing size.
- Keep 10%+ margins. A poster with breathing room beats a cramped one every time.

## YouTube Thumbnails

- Size: 1280 × 720.
- **3 words max** on the thumbnail. Huge, high-contrast type.
- One focal point: face (expressive) or object or giant number — never all three.
- Accent color must pop against YouTube's white/dark UI. Test at small size: readable at 120px wide or it's wrong.
- No clickbait lies — the thumbnail must match the video.

## Banners

| Platform | Size | Safe zone |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | 1584 × 396 | Center 1200×300 |
| X (Twitter) | 1500 × 500 | Center, avoid edges |
| YouTube | 2560 × 1440 | Center 1546 × 423 (all devices) |

- Left- or center-weighted composition; keep key content inside the safe zone.
- Minimal: name/tagline + one visual motif. Banners are backdrops, not posters.

## Ad Creatives

- Sizes: 1080×1080 (feed), 1080×1920 (story).
- **Hook in the first glance:** headline ≤ 5 words, readable in 1 second.
- Single CTA, always: one button-style pill, accent color.
- Design 3 variants (different hooks) — testing beats guessing.

## Infographics

- One narrative flow: top-to-bottom or left-to-right, numbered steps.
- Consistent icon style throughout (all line icons or all solid — never mixed).
- Data first: every number verified, sources in small mono at the bottom.
- Max 5–7 data points. More than that → it's a report, not an infographic.
